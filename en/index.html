How it works

Three simple steps

1

Photograph the document

Invoice or receipt. Long documents in multiple photos. Or scan a QR code.

2

Review and confirm

The app detects supplier, amount and date. You quickly review and confirm.

3

Send to your accountant

At month end, send all documents in one tap. Email opens with the attachment ready.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Scan2Accountant for? +
Freelancers, small companies and anyone who wants to save time on monthly paperwork for their accountant.
Is my data safe? +
Yes. All data stays on your phone. No server registration and no forced cloud account.
Does it work offline? +
Yes, almost everything works offline. Internet is only needed for some online checks.
What subscriptions are available? +
After a 30-day trial: Yearly Premium for €9.99 (50 documents / year) or unlimited monthly for €4.99. Pay via Google Play or the App Store; cancel anytime.
Which languages are supported? +
Slovak, Czech, German and English.
